Luke Stewart

I am a second year PhD student in Biostatistics at the University of Pennsylvania and a member of the Mitra Lab. Prior to that I was a research assistant at the Federal Reserve Bank of Boston working with Chris Foote.

Research

My interests are constantly evolving, but these days I'm focusing on:

  • Causal inference in complex, longitudinal settings (primarily using EHR and claims data)
  • Health policy evaluation
  • Simulation frameworks to validate causal inference methods
